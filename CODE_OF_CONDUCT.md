# Code of Conduct

Everyone participating in Fire Emblem Heroes Rerun Schedule is expected to help keep the community welcoming and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect, regardless of their background, identity, experience, or opinions.
- Give constructive feedback about the work, and accept feedback in good faith.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project and respect others' time and boundaries.
- Credit contributors and respect intellectual property and privacy.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexual harassment, deliberate disruption, spam, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable. Do not share account credentials or private user data.

## Scope

These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces, as well as when representing the project publicly.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns to the project maintainer, Clustifle. Public issues may be used for non-sensitive concerns; do not post private information or sensitive incident details publicly. For sensitive matters, use a private contact method published by the maintainer, if available.

The maintainer will assess reports fairly and limit disclosure to what is needed to resolve the concern. Depending on severity and repetition, responses may include a warning, removal of content, restrictions on participation, or a temporary or permanent ban. Serious incidents may warrant immediate restrictions.

Maintainers are also expected to follow this Code of Conduct.
